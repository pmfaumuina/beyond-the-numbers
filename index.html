About the project

Why We Made This

The Human Pulse is a university group research project. We wanted to understand how continuous glucose monitors, insulin pumps, mobile health apps, and artificial intelligence are changing everyday life for people managing diabetes.

Technology can make care more precise and more convenient. It can also create new stress, new privacy risks, and new barriers for people who can't afford it or weren't considered when it was designed. We think both sides deserve an honest conversation.

Each episode combines research with lived experience and short interviews with healthcare professionals and people managing chronic conditions. Throughout, we keep human connection, ethics, privacy, accessibility, and affordability at the center.

This podcast is educational and does not replace professional medical judgment.

Using Digital Health Tools Safely

  • Talk with your care team before changing treatment based on app or device data.
  • Learn what your alerts mean, and set thresholds with your clinician.
  • Read privacy policies and review which apps can access your health data.
  • Keep software up to date and use a passcode on your phone.
  • Have a backup plan if a device fails, a battery dies, or you lose signal.
  • Treat AI suggestions as information to discuss, not as a diagnosis.
